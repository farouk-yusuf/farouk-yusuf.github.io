What this dashboard covers

Birmingham City Council publishes every payment to suppliers over £500. This dashboard uses the combined extract from 1 July 2024 to 23 July 2026: 247,785 payment lines, including 422 credits (refunds and reversals). All totals are net of those credits.

How the comparison works

The data stops on 23 July 2026, so the spending pages compare 1 April to 23 July 2026 with the same dates in 2025. Comparing a part-month with a full month would understate this year.

Departments

Directorate names changed in 2025 and contain spelling variants, so each is mapped to one reporting name. Council Management, Finance, HR and Strategy are combined as "Corporate services" to keep the comparison consistent across changed corporate labels.

Potential duplicates

A pair is flagged when the same supplier received the same amount (£1,000 or more) within three days, and that amount appears exactly twice for that supplier across the extract. Without the "exactly twice" rule, the first screen returned 6,553 pairs, including recurring care-package amounts. Review IDs and category labels match the supporting AP review workbook; a pair can span more than one category, so both underlying records need checking.

Limits and privacy

  • Payments made are not budgets, commitments or full service costs, so a rise is not evidence of an overspend.
  • Payments are recorded by payment date, so timing (for example, contract schedules) can move year-to-date figures.
  • About a third of 2025/26 value sits in a generic "PO accrual" category, which limits category analysis.
  • The source contains no invoice references, so flagged pairs need internal checks.
  • Individuals' names are redacted in the source. Here, their payments are pooled as "Individuals (names withheld)". Supplier numbers that appear on any redacted payment are pooled the same way, and no supplier numbers are shown.

Source and tools

Birmingham City Council, Payments to Suppliers over £500, published on the Birmingham City Observatory under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Cleaned in Python and aggregated for this page. Categories outside the fifteen named groups are combined as “All other categories”. Review-category filters use the AP workbook label. No figures are estimated.
