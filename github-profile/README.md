## What I bring to an analysis

- **A clear brief:** define the business question, stakeholders, reporting scope and evidence needed.
- **Reliable data:** clean inconsistent records, reconcile totals and make limitations visible.
- **Relevant methods:** combine SQL, statistics and quantitative analysis with interviews, feedback and text analysis.
- **Useful reporting:** explain the finding, show the evidence and distinguish a recommendation from a measured outcome.
- **Human oversight of AI:** use LLMs to support text analysis and development, then check their outputs against evidence.

## Selected projects

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farouk-yusuf/farouk-yusuf.github.io/main/assets/logos/bcc.png" alt="Birmingham City Council" height="28"> Council spending and payment controls

**[Explaining expenses and flagging payments for review](https://farouk-yusuf.github.io/case-studies/birmingham-payments.html)**

I analysed **247,785 payment records**, standardised department names and built a SQL reporting model. The refined screening identified **281 potential duplicate payment pairs worth £4.45m** for investigation—not confirmed losses or recovered savings.

*SQL · Python · Excel · Reconciliation · Exception reporting*

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farouk-yusuf/farouk-yusuf.github.io/main/assets/logos/platform.png" alt="Platform Housing Group" height="28"> Tenant experience and service performance

**[Finding the service gaps behind tenant satisfaction](https://farouk-yusuf.github.io/case-studies/platform-housing.html)**

I benchmarked **12 satisfaction measures** against a cohort of 17 large housing associations, including Platform. Six measures fell in the bottom quarter of the cohort. I combined peer gaps with service-score associations to recommend focused reviews of listening, communication and maintenance.

*Python · Excel · Benchmarking · Statistics · Service insight*

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farouk-yusuf/farouk-yusuf.github.io/main/assets/logos/severn-trent.png" alt="Severn Trent Water" height="28"> Storm-overflow reporting and investigation priorities

**[Checking spill reductions and prioritising overflow investigations](https://farouk-yusuf.github.io/case-studies/severn-trent.html)**

I combined **six years of annual returns**, examined rainfall context and produced separate operational review lists. The maintenance export contains **87 exceptions**, including **27 repeat low-operation records**. I kept observed spill counts separate from model-adjusted screening results.

*Python · Excel · ETL · Data validation · Operational reporting*

### <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farouk-yusuf/farouk-yusuf.github.io/main/assets/logos/nhs.svg" alt="NHS" height="24"> NHS waiting times and recovery planning

**[Explaining waiting-list growth and targeting specialty reviews](https://farouk-yusuf.github.io/case-studies/uhb-waiting-times.html)**

I analysed **41 months of RTT data**, identified a **21,526-pathway balance requiring further reconciliation**, and highlighted different specialty pressures. I tested the planning models against a no-change baseline and reported where the baseline performed better.

*Python · Excel · Time-series analysis · Forecast validation · Performance reporting*

These are independent analyses of published data. The case studies label simulated workplace briefs separately from actual calculations and outputs.
