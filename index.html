02 / Research that shaped my approach

The disagreement changed my research.

During my PhD, I tested human and AI coding before applying it to a corpus of 29,182 cleaned social media posts about leaving Nigeria.

The average looked reassuring. The question-level breakdown told a more complicated story.

I investigated disagreements, revised the coding rules and set aside a finer scheme when the pilot proved unstable.

03 / A little about me

Curiosity, fieldwork

I’m completing a PhD in International Development at the University of Birmingham, with my viva pending. My research examines why skilled Nigerians migrate to the UK.

I taught myself Python during the PhD and learned SQL for the council payments project. I enjoy the point where a technical check changes the explanation you would give someone.

I’m interested in analyst roles in performance reporting, data quality and public-service insight. My toolkit includes SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI, alongside research design and communicating with different audiences.
