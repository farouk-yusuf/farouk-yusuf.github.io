# Birmingham City Council: spend and payment controls summary

*Payments over £500, July 2024 to 23 July 2026 (247,785 payment lines, £3.87bn). Full tables in `BCC_Spend_and_Payment_Controls_Pack.xlsx`.*

## Spend
- **FY2025/26 outturn: £1.81bn** across 110,426 payments to 4,555 suppliers. Adult Social Care & Health is the largest area (28%).
- **Year to date (1 Apr to 23 Jul) spend is 21% higher than the same period last year** (£677m vs £559m). The largest single driver is Birmingham Highways (the highways PFI contract): £88m paid this year against £21m last year, partly offset by a £12m credit, so likely a timing difference to confirm with City Operations.
- 35% of FY2025/26 spend is recorded under "PO accrual" rather than a real spend category, which limits category reporting.

## Suppliers
- The top 10 suppliers received 40% of spend; the top 25, 54%.
- **Agency staff: £101m in FY2025/26, and up 29% year to date** (£43m vs £34m). One agency, Hays, accounts for 84% of it.

## Payment control exceptions (for Accounts Payable)
- **281 possible duplicate payments worth £4.45m:** the same one-off amount paid to the same supplier twice within 3 days (73 on the same day). The published data has no invoice reference, so each needs checking against the finance system; some may be publication duplicates.
- **260 suppliers appear under more than one supplier number** (e.g. "T C Training Ltd" / "T.C. Training Limited"). Duplicate supplier records are a known route to duplicate payments.
- 422 credits (£25.8m) and 995 payments over £500k listed for review.

## Personal data (for Information Governance)
- 7,681 payments to individuals (£24.5m) have the name redacted, **but their supplier numbers are still published**, linking all payments to the same person.
- **12 of those supplier numbers also appear elsewhere in the file with a name**, so 213 redacted payments can be re-identified. In this analysis those numbers are pseudonymised and no names are reproduced.

## Data quality
Directorates were renamed in September 2025 and budgets moved between corporate directorates, so 18 source names were mapped to reporting groups and corporate directorates compared as one. Full log in the workbook.

## Recommended actions
1. AP to check the 281 possible duplicates, starting with the 73 same-day pairs.
2. Merge or close duplicate supplier records.
3. City Operations to confirm the Birmingham Highways timing before the year-to-date position is reported.
4. Information Governance to review publication of supplier numbers for redacted payees.
