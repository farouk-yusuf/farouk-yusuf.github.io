# Requirements brief: council spend and payment controls

**Stakeholders (assumed):** Head of Financial Control; Accounts Payable manager; Information Governance lead.

**Questions**
1. What did the council spend in FY2025/26, and how is this year tracking against last year, by directorate?
2. Which suppliers and spend areas dominate, and how is agency staff spend moving?
3. Which payments should AP check as possible duplicates, and is the supplier master clean?
4. Does the published transparency data protect individuals' personal data?

**Measures:** net spend (£), payment counts, year-to-date change, supplier share of spend, agency spend, count/value of exceptions.
**Data:** Birmingham City Council "Payments to Suppliers over £500" (City Observatory, OGL), Jul 2024 to Jul 2026.
**Out of scope:** budgets (not published), invoice-level matching (no invoice references), payments under £500.
**Deliverables:** SQL database and queries, Excel controls pack, management summary, data-quality log.
