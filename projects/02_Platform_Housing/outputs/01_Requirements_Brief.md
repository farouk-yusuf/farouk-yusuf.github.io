# Requirements brief: annual TSM benchmarking

**Stakeholder (assumed):** Director of Customer Experience, Platform Housing Group, preparing the annual TSM report to the board.
**Questions**
1. How does Platform compare with similar landlords on each Tenant Satisfaction Measure, and how has it moved since last year?
2. Which measures matter most for overall satisfaction, and where should improvement effort go?
3. How does Platform's complaint outcome record at the Housing Ombudsman compare with Midlands landlords?
4. Are any year-on-year movements caused by data or recording changes rather than performance?
**Peer groups:** housing associations with 40,000+ homes (primary); Midlands housing associations (secondary); 20 Midlands landlords with Ombudsman reports.
**Data:** RSH TSM full datasets 2023/24 and 2024/25; Housing Ombudsman landlord reports 2025/26 (PDF, extracted).
**Out of scope:** tenant-level survey responses (not published), cost of improvements.
