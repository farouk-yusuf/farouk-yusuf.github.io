# Storm overflow performance 2025: management summary

*Prepared from the published 2025 Environment Agency annual return. Full tables are in `Severn_Trent_Overflow_Performance_Pack_2025.xlsx`. Read the [evidence checks and interpretation](05_Website_Validation_Notes.md) alongside the preserved workbook.*

## Performance
| | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total spills | 62,085 | 36,471 | -41% |
| Spill hours | 454,155 | 200,361 | -56% |
| Overflows with 60+ spills | 312 | 76 | -76% |
| Monitors working ≥90% of year | 91.3% | 96.3% | +5.0 pts |
| Midlands rainfall | 1,000 mm | 717 mm | -28% |

2025 was much drier, so the observed decline cannot be attributed entirely to improvement work. A five-observation rainfall model provides exploratory context, not causal attribution. Comparing 2022 with 2025, when annual regional rainfall was similar, average recorded spills per qualifying overflow were 22.7% lower. The qualifying assets and individual storm patterns differ between years.

- **Severn basin** fell further (-52%) than the **Trent basin** (-37%); the Trent basin holds 58 of the 76 high-spilling overflows.
- **Storm tanks at treatment works** are 13% of overflows but produced 47% of 2025 spill hours.

## Return quality checks
The workbook records five passing checks, five review items and one information count. It reports zero duplicate IDs and no missing reasons among low-operation flags. Before formal reporting, review 21 missing spill counts, 90 low-operation flags, 11 new IDs, 18 removed IDs and two sites whose counts rose by at least 50 spills. Structural checks do not constitute service-owner sign-off.

## Actions
1. **Maintenance:** 90 records are flagged below 90% operation. The maintenance export excludes records labelled “No longer operational”, leaving 87 exceptions. Of those, 27 also fell below 90% in 2024. Review their current status first. The main reported reasons are installation/design issues (29), sensor faults (21) and communications faults (18); these are not independently verified root causes.
2. **Investment / operations:** 76 overflows spilled 60+ times even in a dry year. 13 report no improvement action in 2025 and should be reviewed first.
3. **Reporting:** publish 2025 figures alongside rainfall context, so a wetter 2026 isn't read as deterioration.

The separate longer-term screen identifies 74 model-adjusted persistent high-spill sites, 17 reporting no improvement action. It uses a narrower linked panel and different rules from the observed 76-site list; the lists overlap and must not be added. Published reporting-period activity does not establish the current investment programme.
