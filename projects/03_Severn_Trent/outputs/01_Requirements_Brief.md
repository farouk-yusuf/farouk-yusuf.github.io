# Requirements brief: Severn Trent storm overflow performance

**Stakeholder (assumed):** Head of Wastewater Performance, Severn Trent Water
**Decision they face:** where to direct the next round of storm overflow investment, and how to report 2025 performance honestly to the board and regulator.

## Questions
1. Severn Trent's recorded spills fell sharply in 2025. How much of that fall is explained by a drier year, and how much is consistent with genuine improvement?
2. Has monitoring reliability changed over time, and does that distort year-on-year comparisons?
3. Which overflows spill heavily every year even after allowing for rainfall, and are they receiving improvement work?

## Measures
- Total spills and spill hours per year (EA 12/24-hour counting method)
- Average spills per overflow, using only monitors working at least 90% of the year
- Share of monitors working less than 90% of the year
- Rainfall-adjusted spills per overflow (scaled to a typical wet year of 1,000 mm)

## Data
- Environment Agency Event Duration Monitoring annual returns, 2020-2025 (overflow level)
- Environment Agency long-term trends summary tables (company level)
- Met Office HadUK-Grid annual rainfall, Midlands and England

## Out of scope
- Spill volumes (not published), water-quality impact, costs of improvement schemes
- Event-level timing of spills (only annual totals are published)

## Deliverables
Clean overflow-year dataset, priority list of overflows, three charts, findings summary, data-quality log.
