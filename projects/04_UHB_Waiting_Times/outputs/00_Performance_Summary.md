- **UHB exceeded the March references carried in the original pack:** 68.6% under 18 weeks versus a 65% reference, and 0.44% at 52+ weeks versus an under-1% reference. Confirm the applicable requirements against current official NHS planning guidance before making a compliance claim.
- **UHB has the best 18-week performance of the seven large Midlands trusts compared, and is above England (65.2%).** 52-week waits are a tenth of the national rate (0.18% vs 1.57%).

## The risk: performance has slipped since March
- The waiting list has grown 7.5% in five months (89,544 to 96,303) and 18-week performance has fallen 2 points.
- **Additions increased more than recorded clock stops.** Starts were 15.2% higher in April–August 2026 than a year earlier (133,091 vs 115,481), while stops rose 0.8% (104,806 vs 103,997). Stops include treatment and other qualifying endings; these aggregates do not establish unchanged clinical capacity.
- **Starts minus stops does not explain the list change.** The 28,285 net additions exceed the actual 6,759 increase by 21,526 pathways. That unclassified net residual needs coded-removal and reporting-adjustment records before causes can be assigned.
- The original continuation scenario gives **107,190 pathways by March 2027** in the rounded workbook. Holding that model flat would require approximately **1,555 additional clock stops a month**, with all other assumptions unchanged. This is not a feasible capacity target or a count of treatment slots; the model does not project the under-18-week percentage.

## Specialties
- **ENT and Ophthalmology hold 62.9% of the remaining 52+ week pathways** (57 and 53), despite both improving sharply over the year.
- **Gynaecology is deteriorating:** list up 38% in a year and 18-week performance down 11 points to 45.9%, the lowest of the large specialties. **Oral Surgery** is also below 50%.

## Forecast reliability
Both forecasting methods tested (a demand-and-capacity model and a simple trend) missed the turn in March 2026 when back-tested on the last six months; a 'no change' forecast was more accurate (2.5% error vs 6-8%). The March 2027 figure is therefore a scenario if current demand continues, not a prediction. It should be refreshed monthly.

## Data checks
The report records no missing provider-months for the selected organisations across 41 months, and zero unknown clock starts in the UHB trend. The latest 23 specialty rows sum to the trust's 96,303 waiting pathways and 175 at 52+ weeks. Source-row weekly-band totals still require reconciliation to Total All; aggregate checks do not establish complete source coverage.
