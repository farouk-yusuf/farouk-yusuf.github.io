# University Hospitals Birmingham: elective waiting times, August 2026

*NHS England RTT data, April 2023 to August 2026, for UHB (RRK), six large Midlands trusts and England. Full tables in `UHB_RTT_Performance_Report.xlsx`.*

## Where we are
| | Mar 2025 | Mar 2026 | Aug 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting under 18 weeks | 50.2% | 68.6% | 66.6% |
| Waiting 52+ weeks | n/a | 397 (0.44%) | 175 (0.18%) |
| Waiting list | | 89,544 | 96,303 |

- **UHB met its March 2026 milestones:** 68.6% within 18 weeks (target 65%, and well above the required 5-point improvement) and 0.44% waiting over 52 weeks (target under 1%).
- **UHB has the best 18-week performance of the seven large Midlands trusts compared, and is above England (65.2%).** 52-week waits are a tenth of the national rate (0.18% vs 1.57%).

## The risk: performance has slipped since March
- The waiting list has grown 7.5% in five months (89,544 to 96,303) and 18-week performance has fallen 2 points.
- **The cause is demand, not capacity.** Referrals (clock starts) were 15% higher in April to August 2026 than a year earlier (133,091 vs 115,481), while treatments and discharges (clock stops) were flat (104,806 vs 103,997).
- If that continues, the list reaches about **107,000 by March 2027**. Holding it flat would need roughly **1,550 extra clock stops a month (about 7% more activity)**. This puts the 70% milestone for March 2027 at risk.

## Specialties
- **ENT and Ophthalmology hold 63% of the remaining 52-week waiters** (57 and 53 patients), despite both improving sharply over the year.
- **Gynaecology is deteriorating:** list up 38% in a year and 18-week performance down 11 points to 45.9%, the lowest of the large specialties. **Oral Surgery** is also below 50%.

## Forecast reliability
Both forecasting methods tested (a demand-and-capacity model and a simple trend) missed the turn in March 2026 when back-tested on the last six months; a 'no change' forecast was more accurate (2.5% error vs 6-8%). The March 2027 figure is therefore a scenario if current demand continues, not a prediction. It should be refreshed monthly.

## Data checks
No missing submissions for UHB, its peers or England across 41 months; no patients with unknown clock-start dates.
