# Requirements brief: monthly elective waiting times report

**Stakeholder (assumed):** Chief Operating Officer and divisional directors, University Hospitals Birmingham.
**Questions**
1. Is the trust on track for the national 18-week and 52-week milestones?
2. Why is the waiting list moving: more referrals, or less activity?
3. Which specialties hold the long waits, and which are getting worse?
4. How does UHB compare with similar Midlands trusts and England?
5. Where is the waiting list heading if current trends continue?
**Data:** NHS England RTT full monthly extracts (41 months), commissioner rows summed to trust level.
**Measures:** waiting list (incomplete pathways), % within 18 weeks, 52/65/78-week waiters, clock starts, clock stops.
**Out of scope:** patient-level data, cancer and diagnostic waits, A&E.
